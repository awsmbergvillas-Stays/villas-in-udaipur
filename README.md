## Planning your Udaipur stay

- **Best time to visit:** October to March has the most comfortable weather.
- **Areas to consider:** the Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar side for views, and the outskirts for larger properties with lawns and more privacy.
- **Booking tip:** book early for the wedding season and winter holidays.
