## Good occasions for a villa stay

- **Family holidays:** several generations under one roof, with room to relax together.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries:** a private setting for dinners, music and decorations.
- **Small weddings and pre-wedding events:** haldi, mehendi, sangeet and family gatherings.
- **Friends' getaways:** shared stays that work out cheaper per person than separate hotel rooms.
- **Team offsites:** a quiet place to work and unwind together.

## Questions to ask before you book

1. How many guests can the villa sleep, and how many can it host for the day?
2. Are events, music and outside caterers allowed, and until what time?
3. Is there parking for all your vehicles?
4. What is included: housekeeping, a cook, breakfast, power backup?
5. Are there extra charges for additional guests or for events?
6. What are the cancellation and advance payment terms?

## Tips for Udaipur

- **Book early** for October to March, when weather and wedding demand are at their peak.
- **Confirm the location** with the host. Properties near the lakes and in the outskirts offer very different experiences.
- **Plan transport** in advance for large groups, since lanes near the old city can be narrow.
